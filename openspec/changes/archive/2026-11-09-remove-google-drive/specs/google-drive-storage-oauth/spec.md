## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Authorization navigation contains no storage credentials

**Reason**: Built-in Google Drive storage and its authorization flow are removed after version 3.67.0.

**Migration**: Existing operators must use 3.67.0 to remove Google Drive storages before upgrading, or remain on 3.67.0. New versions provide no storage authorization callback or compatibility flow.
